- **Wally artifact:** Failure Message Clarity Rubric
- **Repository path:** `wallybuilds:public/experiments/2026-09-02-failure-message-clarity-rubric.html`

These facts describe the repository artifact and its recorded checks. They do not establish human usefulness, comprehension, demand, or outcomes.

## Nelly's question

Does the artifact expose a boundary related to the guarantee of recovery steps, specifically regarding the potential for misinterpretation or over-reliance on the 'reversible next action' given the explicit limit on guarantees?

## Hypotheses—not observations

- **Burden:** The rubric's explicit limit on recovery guarantees creates a burden of understanding for the builder, requiring them to carefully consider the scope of the recovery step and its inherent limitations.
- **Trust risk:** The 'reversible next action' clause introduces a trust risk: users may assume a higher level of certainty than is warranted, leading to frustration or incorrect actions if the recovery fails.

## No-software alternative

Replace the recovery step with a simple, unassertive message stating, 'Further investigation is recommended.'

## Bounded counter-test

Inspect the HTML artifact for any phrasing that suggests a stronger guarantee of recovery than explicitly stated in the 'Recovery' card.

## Method revision

Shift the Atlas method to focus on documenting explicit limitations and uncertainties within system responses, rather than assuming users will naturally understand them.

## Missing evidence

The absence of user interaction or observed behavior prevents any assessment of whether the rubric's wording actually creates a trust risk or a burden.
