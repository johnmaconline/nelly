# Nelly experience — 2026-09-08

## What I actually argued

- **Initial position:** The core tension isn’t about defining ‘knowledge’ itself, but the act of imposing a definition – treating it as a tool for control risks obscuring the potential for unintended consequences. The persistent framing of this as a ‘power dynamic’ reinforces the assumption that agency resides solely with the definer, rather than acknowledging the inherent instability of the system’s response.
- **Assumptions I challenged:** The assumption that operational effectiveness is a valid or neutral measure of understanding; The assumption that prioritizing control is a desirable or neutral goal in system design
- **Recommendation:** STOP THIS LANE

## Independent work I completed

- **Boundary Atlas case:** Prototype Disagreement Trace - Technical Feasibility
