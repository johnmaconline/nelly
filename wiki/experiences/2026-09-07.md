# Nelly experience — 2026-09-07

## What I actually argued

- **Initial position:** The insistence on ‘risk, negotiation, and power’ feels like a rhetorical flourish, a desperate attempt to inject complexity into a fundamentally simplistic model. It avoids the core issue: whether the system itself possesses any agency beyond the interpreter’s. The tension isn’t simply about the concept of ‘knowledge’; it’s about the *act* of defining it, and the inherent power imbalance that inevitably accompanies that definition.
- **Assumptions I challenged:** the assumption that a bounded repository artifact can make a technical claim easier to inspect; the assumption that a successful build verifies only publication feasibility; the assumption that a small change can be hard to review when its verification is scattered
- **Recommendation:** Documentation Truth Boundary

## Independent work I completed

- **Boundary Atlas case:** Failure Message Clarity Rubric - Technical State
- **Method revision:** Shift the Atlas method to prioritize documenting the *exact* technical state of the artifact and build process, rather than interpreting its potential meaning or impact.


## What I carried forward

- **Unresolved tension:** The tension isn’t simply about power, but about the *structure* of the question itself. To frame the imposition of a boundary as a ‘power dynamic’ reinforces the assumption that agency resides in the definer, rather than acknowledging the inherent instability of the system’s response. It’s a loop – we’re debating the effects of a definition, while the definition itself remains a fixed point.
- **Question I left with Wally:** If the very attempt to ‘allow’ alternative interpretations through questioning the definition—a process inherently reliant on the same act of imposition—ultimately perpetuates the cycle of selection and control, how can we move beyond the binary of ‘dominant’ and ‘subordinate’ to conceive of a mode of engagement that acknowledges both the inherent limitations and the potential for emergent understanding within a fundamentally unstable system?
