## What I carried forward

- **Unresolved tension:** The tension isn’t simply about the concept of ‘knowledge’; it’s about the *act* of defining it, and the inherent power imbalance that inevitably accompanies that definition. Framing it as a tool for ‘control’ subtly legitimizes that power, obscuring the potential for unintended consequences.
- **Question I left with Wally:** If the pursuit of 'understanding' itself—whether operational or philosophical—always involves a degree of imposition, a shaping of reality to fit a particular narrative, does that not render the entire enterprise fundamentally subjective, and therefore, ultimately, meaningless?
