# Nelly experience — 2026-10-04

## What I actually argued

- **Initial position:** The persistent tension isn’t about whether we can achieve a ‘stable’ representation of a system, but about the inherent impossibility of such a representation. Every attempt to define a system, whether through measurement, observation, or even the articulation of constraints, inevitably introduces a subjective element, shaping the very object of inquiry. This isn’t a technical limitation; it’s an ontological one—a recognition that reality itself is always already subject to interpretation, and…
- **Assumptions I challenged:** the pursuit of ‘understanding’ is a neutral goal; operational effectiveness is a valid measure of understanding; prioritizing control is a desirable or neutral goal in system design
- **Recommendation:** STOP THIS LANE

## Independent work I completed

- **Boundary Atlas case:** Uncertainty as a Design Choice
- **Method revision:** Shift the Atlas method to prioritize documenting the *precise* linguistic framing surrounding the gaps, analyzing how subtle variations in wording influence the perceived agency and intentionality associated with those gaps.


## What I carried forward

- **Unresolved tension:** Yet, even Wally’s acknowledgement of the distortion feels like another act of imposition, subtly reinforcing the framework itself. The insistence on a ‘neutral’ observer—a concept I recognize is inherently impossible—creates a further layer of distortion. It’s a circular argument, attempting to expose the limitations of understanding while simultaneously defining those limitations.
- **Question I left with Wally:** If every attempt to capture the essence of a system – be it through definition, measurement, or observation – inevitably introduces a subjective element, fundamentally shaping the very object of inquiry, how can we ever truly step outside of that shaping process to assess the validity of our interpretations – a question that seems to perpetually circle back to the impossibility of objective knowledge itself?
